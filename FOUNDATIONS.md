## Standards

**1. Never mention a failed payment before acknowledging the member's year or goals.**
- *In action:* Sakita's renewal payment didn't go through. I led with her salon idea and excitement for her 2026-2027 year, then raised the payment.
- *Wrong answer:* "Hi Sakita, your payment failed. Please update your card." Accurate, but it skips the relationship and reads like a billing notice.

**2. Every follow-up references something specific from the last conversation.**
- *In action:* In the same email, I referenced Sakita mentioning she wanted to meet with Coco and offered to set up and sit in on the call.
- *Wrong answer:* "Following up on our conversation. Let me know if you need anything." Generic, with no detail and no clear ask.

**3. Confirm a member's intent before acting on what the system shows.**
- *In action:* The system showed Erica had opted out of organization-wide communications. I asked her to confirm it was intentional rather than assume.
- *Wrong answer:* Removing her from all outreach without asking, or writing "We noticed you unsubscribed by mistake."

## Stop and bring me in
- Any time a member asks for something that needs an exception, a discount, or a decision outside my authority.
- Any time a member detail can't be verified.

## Decision sorting

**Delegate**
- Prepping notes before member calls, with sources cited
- Suggesting my daily task priorities and what can wait

**Delegate with review** (I check every item for voice, accuracy, verified member details, and one clear ask)
- Drafting renewal emails
- Drafting follow-ups after member calls
- Drafting onboarding emails
- Drafting replies to payment issues
- Scheduling calls with members
- Facilitating intros (like Sakita to Coco)

**Never delegate**
- Approving discounts or exceptions (my manager decides)
- Escalating to my manager

## The guardrail I won't relax
- Never get a member's details wrong because of old Salesforce data. Before using any member detail, check it against the most recent emails and internal notes. If sources conflict, flag the conflict and leave a placeholder instead of picking one.
